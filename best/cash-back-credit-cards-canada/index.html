Best Cash Back Credit Cards in Canada

Cash back cards are the simplest reward in personal finance: spend money, get a percentage back, no points conversion required. The right card depends on where you spend the most — the best grocery earn rate does nothing for you if your biggest monthly expense is gas. We ranked these six cards on net annual value for a typical Canadian household.

Top 6 Cash Back Credit Cards for Canadians

Annual fees and earn rates current as of 2026. Always confirm terms with the issuer before applying.

  • 1
    American Express SimplyCash Preferred Card
    4% cash back on groceries and gas (up to $300 back per year on each), 2% on everything else with no cap. Straightforward earn structure with no rotating categories. A strong pick for households with high grocery and fuel spending.
    $99/yr
  • 2
    Scotia Momentum Visa Infinite
    4% cash back on grocery and recurring bill payments, 2% on gas and daily transit, 1% everywhere else. The recurring bills category is unique and valuable — most Canadian households have several monthly subscriptions that qualify.
    $120/yr
  • 3
    BMO Cashback World Elite Mastercard
    5% on groceries, 4% on transit, 3% on gas and EV charging, 2% on recurring bills, 1% on everything else. Highest grocery earn rate of any major Canadian cash back card. Redeem any time with a $1 minimum — no annual payout waiting period.
    $120/yr
  • 4
    Tangerine Money-Back Credit Card
    2% cash back on up to three categories of your choice (groceries, gas, restaurants, drugstores, entertainment, and more), 0.5% on everything else. No annual fee. Ideal for those who want flexibility without paying a yearly fee.
    No annual fee
  • 5
    Rogers Red Mastercard
    3% unlimited cash back on all US dollar purchases, 1.5% on everything else in Canadian dollars. No annual fee. The 3% USD rate effectively eliminates the 2.5% foreign transaction fee and adds a small gain — an exceptional card for online USD spending and cross-border shopping.
    No annual fee
  • 6
    TD Cash Back Visa Infinite
    3% cash back on groceries, gas, and recurring bill payments. 1% on all other purchases. Includes a comprehensive insurance package — emergency travel medical, auto rental collision, and purchase protection — making it competitive beyond just the earn rate.
    $139/yr
