How much is an RBC Avion point worth?

It depends on your Avion membership level, which comes from the RBC card you hold. RBC says Avion Elite and Avion Premium members can redeem points for any type of travel at 100 points per $1, which is 1 cent per point. Avion Select members, and RBC ION and ION+ cardholders, redeem at 172 points per $1, about 0.58 cents.

CardGenie's 1 cent baseline is the Elite rate on any travel, which a holder of an RBC Avion Visa Infinite can reach without planning anything. In the card list below, the RBC ION+ Visa is valued at 0.8 cents, because its points reach the full Avion menu only when moved to an Avion card. The ION+ and Avion Visa Infinite guide explains that pairing.

Transfer partners

The Avion Rewards terms allow conversions to WestJet points at 1 to 1, British Airways Avios at 1 to 1, Cathay Asia Miles at 1 to 1 and American Airlines AAdvantage miles at 10 points to 7 miles. Avion Select members are not eligible for airline conversions. The terms summary sets minimums of 1,000 points for WestJet, 5,000 for AAdvantage and 10,000 each for Avios and Asia Miles, and conversions cannot be reversed. Points can also convert to Canadian Tire Money at 140 points for $1, about 0.71 cents per point.

Avion Rewards Pay, which spends points at checkout, gives 10,000 points for $58 on most Avion cards and $100 on Avion Privilege and Private Banking cards, with limits of $250 per transaction and $1,000 per day.

Do RBC Avion points expire?

RBC says on its Avion Visa Infinite page that Avion points never expire. The Avion Rewards terms add one exception: points are forfeited twelve months after you close your RBC credit card account, or after RBC closes it for any reason other than cause.

Common questions

How much is an RBC Avion point worth?

CardGenie values Avion points at 1 cent each, so 50,000 points is about $500. That is the rate Avion Elite and Premium members get on any travel, at 100 points per $1. Avion Select members and ION or ION+ cardholders redeem travel at 172 points per $1, about 0.58 cents.

Is the Air Travel Redemption Schedule better than booking any travel?

Only when the fare is close to the schedule's maximum ticket price. A 35,000-point Explore North America flight is worth 2 cents per point on a $700 fare, but under 1 cent on a $300 fare, where booking at 100 points per $1 uses fewer points.

Do RBC Avion points expire?

RBC says Avion points never expire while your account is open. If you close your RBC credit card account, or RBC closes it for a reason other than cause, the points are forfeited twelve months later.

Can I transfer RBC Avion points to an airline?

Yes, if you are an Avion Elite or Premium member. Points convert to WestJet points, British Airways Avios and Cathay Asia Miles at 1 to 1, and to American Airlines AAdvantage at 10 points to 7 miles. Avion Select members cannot convert to airlines.
