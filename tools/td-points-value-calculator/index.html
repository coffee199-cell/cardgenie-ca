How much are TD points worth?

A TD Rewards point is worth a different amount depending on how you redeem it. TD's TD Rewards terms say that when you book travel directly through Expedia For TD, every 200 points are worth $1, redeemed in increments of 200 points. That is 0.5 cents per point, and it is CardGenie's baseline.

Outside Expedia For TD, points are redeemed with Pay off Purchases against purchases on your card. TD lists travel at 225 points per $1, about 0.44 cents, and everyday purchases at 400 points per $1, or 0.25 cents, with a $1 minimum. TD says those two values were accurate as of August 19, 2025. On a 100,000-point balance, the three routes give about $500, $444 and $250. Choose a route in the calculator to see your own balance.

Why 8x is not 8 percent

TD Rewards multipliers look large because each point is worth half a cent. The TD First Class Travel Visa Infinite earns 8 points per $1 on travel booked through Expedia For TD, 6 points on groceries, dining and public transit, 4 points on recurring bills and streaming, and 2 points on other purchases. At 0.5 cents per point those are 4, 3, 2 and 1 percent. Its welcome guide caps the 6x and 4x rates at $25,000 of spending a year per category.

The card list above shows travel at the 2x base rate, because only bookings made through Expedia For TD earn 8x and most travel spending is not booked there. If you book through Expedia For TD, that spending returns 4 percent.

Best and worst ways to use TD points

Expedia For TD is the best common redemption, because it pairs the highest earn rate with the best redemption rate. Booking travel somewhere else and covering it with Pay off Purchases costs 225 points per $1 instead of 200, so a $500 trip takes 112,500 points rather than 100,000.

The weakest common choice is using points against everyday purchases, at 400 points per $1, which is half the Expedia For TD value. TD also lists gift cards, merchandise, Amazon and Starbucks among its ways to redeem. Its own pages do not publish a current per-dollar rate for every one of them, so put the points price and the cash price of any offer into the checker above before you redeem.

Transfers and expiry

The TD Rewards terms describe no conversion of points to airline or hotel loyalty programs, so the value of a point is set by TD's own redemption options. Those terms also say points do not expire while the account stays open, active and in good standing. If you close the account, you have 90 days to redeem your points.

Common questions

How much are TD points worth?

CardGenie values TD Rewards points at 0.5 cents each, the rate for travel booked through Expedia For TD at 200 points per $1. So 100,000 points is about $500. Other travel redeems at 225 points per $1 and everyday purchases at 400 points per $1.

How many TD points do I need for $100?

You need 20,000 points for $100 of travel booked through Expedia For TD, 22,500 points for $100 of other travel, and 40,000 points for $100 of everyday purchases.

Is it better to redeem TD points through Expedia For TD?

Yes, for value per point. Expedia For TD gives $1 for every 200 points, while travel booked elsewhere costs 225 points per $1. The TD First Class Travel Visa Infinite also earns 8 points per $1 on Expedia For TD bookings, compared with 2 points on other travel.

Do TD Rewards points expire?

Not while your account stays open, active and in good standing. If you close the account, TD gives you 90 days to redeem the points.
