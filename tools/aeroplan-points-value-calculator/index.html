How much is an Aeroplan point worth?

Aeroplan does not have one fixed cash value. For flights on Air Canada, the airline says the price in points "is related to the price of the ticket in cash", so a cheap fare costs fewer points and a busy travel date costs more. Flights on partner airlines work differently: they cost a fixed number of points based on the distance flown.

CardGenie uses 1.5 cents per point as a conservative middle. It is the figure behind every Aeroplan number on this site, including the card calculator, and it is chosen so a typical member booking economy flights can reach it without specialised knowledge. Your own value comes from the redemption checker above: enter the points price, the cash price of the same flight, and the taxes and fees you still pay on the reward.

Best and worst ways to use Aeroplan points

Flight rewards

Flights are where the cash price you avoid is largest, which is why they usually give the best value per point. Air Canada's flight reward chart lists one-way economy flights within North America on Air Canada starting at 6,000 points for trips up to 500 miles and 17,500 points for trips over 2,750 miles. Those are starting prices, not guarantees, and the chart also publishes median prices that are higher: 13,000 points for the shortest band and 22,900 points for the longest.

Partner flights use the fixed distance-based prices in the same chart. A fixed price is useful when the cash fare on a route is high, because the points cost does not rise with it. That is when the checker above is most likely to show a result well above 1.5 cents.

Hotels, cars, vacations and the eStore

Aeroplan points can also pay for hotels, car rentals and Air Canada Vacations packages, and for merchandise and gift cards in the Aeroplan eStore, which starts at 3,200 points. Aeroplan does not publish a cents-per-point value for any of these. Run the offer through the checker and compare it with a flight reward before you spend a large balance on one.

Transferring points into and out of Aeroplan

Aeroplan lists conversion partners that send points into Aeroplan. They include US bank programs such as Chase Ultimate Rewards and Capital One at 1,000 points to 1,000 Aeroplan points, and hotel programs such as Marriott Bonvoy at 3 points to 1 Aeroplan point and World of Hyatt at 2 points to 1.

Moving points out is a different story. The Aeroplan terms say points cannot be transferred or converted to or from another loyalty currency unless Aeroplan permits it. You can transfer points to another Aeroplan member for a fee.

Do Aeroplan points expire?

Only after inactivity. Air Canada's inactivity policy says points expire if the account has been inactive for 18 months, and earning or redeeming at least one point within 18 months keeps it active. Points do not expire at all while you are a primary TD Aeroplan or CIBC Aeroplan credit cardholder, an American Express Aeroplan Cardmember, or while you hold Aeroplan Elite Status.

Common questions

How much is an Aeroplan point worth?

CardGenie values Aeroplan points at 1.5 cents each, so 10,000 points is about $150 and 50,000 points is about $750. The real figure depends on the redemption. Air Canada prices its own flights from the cash fare, so the same balance can be worth more on an expensive date and less on a cheap one.

How do I convert Aeroplan points to dollars?

Multiply the points by the value per point in cents and divide by 100. At 1.5 cents, 40,000 points is 40,000 x 1.5 / 100 = $600. That dollar figure is an estimate of what the points replace when you redeem them, not a cash balance.

How do I work out what an Aeroplan flight reward is worth?

Take the cash price of the same flight, subtract the taxes and fees you still pay on the reward, divide by the points price and multiply by 100. A $520 fare that costs 25,000 points plus $80 in taxes and fees works out to ($520 - $80) / 25,000 x 100 = 1.76 cents per point.

Do Aeroplan points expire?

Aeroplan points expire after 18 months without earning or redeeming any points. They do not expire while you are a primary TD Aeroplan or CIBC Aeroplan credit cardholder, an American Express Aeroplan Cardmember, or an Aeroplan Elite Status member.
