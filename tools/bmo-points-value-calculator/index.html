How much are BMO Rewards points worth?

BMO's credit card terms set two rates for Pay with Points, which uses points to cover purchases charged to your card. Travel purchases redeem at 150 points per $1, about 0.67 cents per point. All other purchases redeem at 200 points per $1, or 0.5 cents. You need enough points for at least $1. Travel means purchases from merchants BMO classifies as airlines, car rental agencies, hotels and resorts, vacation packages, cruise lines, eligible vacation rentals, passenger railways, timeshares, travel agencies and tour operators.

CardGenie's baseline is 0.7 cents: the travel rate rounded to one decimal place, the same figure the all-programs calculator and the card calculator use. The rounding adds about 5 percent to the dollar figure. Choose "Travel at BMO's exact rate" in the calculator for the unrounded number, which turns 50,000 points into about $333 rather than $350.

Best and worst ways to use BMO Rewards points

Travel is the best fixed-rate option, and you do not need a special booking site for it. BMO's BMO Rewards terms say you can book through any travel provider using your primary BMO credit card and then use Pay with Points. Covering non-travel purchases gives a quarter less per point, so 30,000 points is $200 on a flight but $150 on groceries.

BMO also offers merchandise and gift cards, priced in its online catalogue at bmorewards.com rather than at a fixed rate. You can top up a merchandise reward by paying for the missing points if you already have at least 80 percent of them, but gift cards need the full number of points. BMO also lists contributions to a BMO savings or investment account, and the pages checked for this calculator do not publish a rate for them. For any of these, enter the points price and the cash price in the checker above and compare the result with 0.67 cents.

BMO earn rates in percent

BMO cards advertise large multipliers, so convert them before comparing. At 0.7 cents per point, 5 points per $1 returns 3.5 percent, 3 points returns 2.1 percent and 1 point returns 0.7 percent. The BMO Ascend World Elite Mastercard, for example, earns 5 points per $1 on eligible travel, 3 points on dining, entertainment and recurring bills, and 1 point on everything else. The card list above shows the same conversion for every BMO Rewards card in the database.

Transfers and expiry

BMO's BMO Rewards terms let you give points to another BMO Rewards member, with a minimum of 1,000 points, and points you give cannot be returned. The terms describe no conversion to airline or hotel programs. Points do not expire while your account remains open and in good standing. If the account is cancelled, you have 90 days to redeem the points before they expire.

Common questions

How much are BMO Rewards points worth?

CardGenie values BMO Rewards points at 0.7 cents each, so 50,000 points is about $350. BMO redeems travel purchases at 150 points per $1, about 0.67 cents, and other purchases at 200 points per $1, or 0.5 cents.

How many BMO Rewards points do I need for $100?

You need 15,000 points to cover $100 of travel purchases and 20,000 points to cover $100 of other purchases with Pay with Points.

Do BMO Rewards points expire?

Not while your account remains open and in good standing. If the account is cancelled, you have 90 days to redeem the points before they expire.

Can I transfer BMO Rewards points?

You can give points to another BMO Rewards member, with a minimum of 1,000 points. BMO's terms describe no conversion to airline or hotel programs.
