What PC Optimum points are worth

PC Optimum is a fixed-value program. Its program benefits table says "Every 10,000 points is like $10 of free stuff", and PC Financial says that once you accumulate 10,000 points you can spend them on $10 worth of free stuff at participating stores. That is 0.1 cents per point, the figure CardGenie uses everywhere.

The large numbers in PC Optimum offers are a result of that low value, not unusual generosity. A card earning 10 points per dollar returns 1 percent, and an offer of 20,000 bonus points is worth $20. Convert any offer at 0.1 cents before comparing it with a cash back card. The same arithmetic works for earn rates: the card list below shows each card's points per dollar next to the percentage it returns, so a grocery bonus that sounds large can be compared directly with a cash back card's rate.

Where and how you can redeem

The benefits table lists redemption at Shoppers Drug Mart and Pharmaprix, at grocery stores including Loblaws, No Frills, Real Canadian Superstore, Maxi and Provigo, and through Click and Collect and joefresh.ca. PC Financial says you can redeem as soon as you have 10,000 points, for up to $10 worth of free stuff. The calculator above tells you how far your balance is from that minimum.

Best and worst ways to use PC Optimum points

Because the regular rate is fixed, the only common way to beat it is a redemption event. The benefits table describes these as "Spend your Points Events", with examples such as spending 10,000 points for $15 of free stuff. That is 0.15 cents per point, half as much again as the regular rate. Choose the event option above to see what your balance would be worth at that rate, or enter a different offer in the checker.

The main way to lose value is to leave points unused for too long, covered below. Holding a balance also gains you nothing, because the regular rate is the same whether you redeem 10,000 points or 100,000.

Transfers and households

PC Financial says points are not transferable between members. You can, however, form a PC Optimum household and pool points with up to 9 other members, for a total of 10.

Do PC Optimum points expire?

Not on a fixed date, but inactivity counts. PC Financial says your account can be closed if you do not earn or spend any PC Optimum points at least once every 2 years. Any purchase that earns points keeps the account active.

Common questions

How much are PC Optimum points worth?

CardGenie values PC Optimum points at 0.1 cents each, the regular rate of 10,000 points for $10. So 20,000 points is $20, 50,000 points is $50 and 100,000 points is $100.

How many PC Optimum points do I need to redeem?

You need at least 10,000 points, which can be redeemed for $10 worth of free stuff at participating stores.

Do PC Optimum points expire?

PC Financial says your account can be closed if you do not earn or spend any points at least once every 2 years. Earning points on any purchase keeps it active.

Can I transfer PC Optimum points to someone else?

Not between members. You can pool points with up to 9 other members in a PC Optimum household.
