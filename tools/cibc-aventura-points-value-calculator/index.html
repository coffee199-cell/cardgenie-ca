Why CardGenie values Aventura points at 1 cent

CIBC does not publish one cash value for an Aventura point. Its Aventura terms say the points-to-dollars ratio "is determined by CIBC and can be changed at any time", and its rewards FAQ says the rate "varies, depending on transaction type." The figures CIBC does publish set a range:

  • Payment with Points: 4,000 points for a $25 payment on your Aventura card balance, which is 0.625 cents per point. This is the floor for a cash-like redemption.
  • CIBC financial products: 12,000 points for a $100 payment or contribution to an existing CIBC mortgage, TFSA, RRSP or loan, about 0.83 cents per point.
  • The no-fee CIBC Aventura Visa describes its 12,500-point welcome offer as "up to $125 in travel value", which is 1 cent per point.
  • The Aventura Airline Rewards Chart, where a flight priced at the maximum covered fare is worth between 1.6 and 2.3 cents per point at the top of each points range.

CardGenie uses 1 cent. It is CIBC's own travel-value figure on its entry card, it sits above the cash-like floor, and it is well below what the chart gives at its best, so it is a value a typical cardholder redeeming for travel can reach. It also matches the baseline for RBC Avion, the closest comparable bank travel program. The figure is new: it has been added to the all-programs points value calculator and the card calculator so all three agree.

The Aventura Airline Rewards Chart

For flights, CIBC publishes a fixed chart for round-trip economy tickets. The points needed fall within a range for each region, and they cover the base fare up to a maximum before taxes and other charges.

DestinationPoints neededMaximum ticket price
Canada and US, short haul10,000 to 20,000$400
Canada and US, long haul25,000 to 35,000$800
Mexico, Alaska, Hawaii, Central America, Caribbean40,000 to 60,000$1,000
Europe50,000 to 70,000$1,300
Rest of the world75,000 to 125,000$2,000

If the base fare is above the maximum, CIBC says you pay the excess with your Aventura card or with more points. As with any fixed chart, the value comes from fares close to the maximum: 20,000 points on a $400 short-haul fare is 2 cents per point, while the same 20,000 points on a $250 fare is 1.25 cents. Enter the base fare and points in the checker above to test your own flight. CIBC's FAQ lists the cards the chart applies to, including the Aventura Visa Infinite, Visa Infinite Privilege and Gold Visa, so confirm your card is on it.

Other travel booked through the CIBC Rewards Centre can be paid for with any number of points, up to 100 percent of the cost, with no minimum. CIBC does not publish a fixed rate for it, so compare the points price with the cash price in the checker.

Transfers and expiry

The Aventura terms allow a conversion to Aeroplan only for eligible accounts issued before October 1, 2013, at 1 to 1 in increments of 10,000 points. CIBC says Aventura points do not expire. If you close your card you have 60 days to redeem the points, and if CIBC closes the account the points are forfeited immediately.

Common questions

How much is a CIBC Aventura point worth?

CardGenie values Aventura points at 1 cent each, so 50,000 points is about $500 in travel. CIBC does not publish a single rate. Payment with Points gives 0.625 cents per point, and a flight on the Airline Rewards Chart priced near the maximum fare can give 2 cents or more.

What is the best way to redeem Aventura points?

Flights on the Aventura Airline Rewards Chart, when the base fare is close to the chart's maximum ticket price for that region. Paying down your card balance with points gives the least, at 4,000 points for $25.

Do CIBC Aventura points expire?

CIBC says Aventura points do not expire. If you close your card, you have 60 days to redeem them.

Can I transfer Aventura points to Aeroplan?

Only if your Aventura account was issued before October 1, 2013. Those accounts can convert at 1 to 1 in increments of 10,000 points. Newer accounts cannot.
