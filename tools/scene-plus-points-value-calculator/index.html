How much are Scene points worth?

Most Scene+ redemptions use a fixed rate of 100 points for $1, which is 1 cent per point. Scene+ lists that rate for bookings through Scene+ Travel and for movie tickets, food and drinks at Cineplex, and it lists 1,000 points for $10 off at participating grocery stores and pharmacies and at Home Hardware, which is the same rate.

Scotiabank cardholders have two more options. Apply Points to Travel covers flights, hotels, resorts, car rentals and transit charged to a linked Scotiabank card, at 100 points for $1, for up to a year after the purchase. Points for Credit, which turns points into a credit on the card balance, pays 150 points for $1, or about 0.67 cents per point.

That makes Scene+ one of the few Canadian programs where CardGenie's baseline is simply the program's own published rate. There is little upside above 1 cent, and the only common way below it is choosing the statement credit. On a 50,000-point balance the gap is real: about $500 at the standard rate against about $333 as a statement credit. Best and worst Scene+ redemptions

Because travel, groceries and movies all redeem at 1 cent, the best redemption is whichever of them you would have paid cash for anyway. Apply Points to Travel is the most flexible of the travel options, because it works on travel booked anywhere and paid with your Scotiabank card rather than only through the Scene+ Travel site, and Scene+ Travel itself lets you redeem from as little as one point.

The weakest common option is Points for Credit, which gives a third less per point. If you have travel or groceries coming up, applying the points there instead turns 15,000 points into $150 rather than $100. A few smaller limits apply: Cineplex redemptions cannot be put toward taxes, gratuities or alcohol, and gift cards start at 1,000 points. For anything priced in points rather than at a fixed rate, such as a gift card offer, use the checker above to compare it with the 1 cent baseline.

Can you transfer Scene+ points?

No. Scotiabank's Scene+ FAQ says "Scene+ points are non-transferable."

Do Scene+ points expire?

Not while you keep an eligible Scotiabank product open. Scotiabank's Scene+ program page says points do not expire as long as that product remains open. If you close it you remain a Scene+ member, and points expire only after 24 months of inactivity.

Common questions

How much are Scene points worth?

CardGenie values Scene+ points at 1 cent each, the standard Scene+ rate of 100 points for $1, so 10,000 points is $100 and 50,000 points is $500. That rate applies to travel, groceries at participating stores and movies at Cineplex. Points for Credit on a Scotiabank card pays less, at 150 points for $1.

What is the best way to use Scene+ points?

Use them at 1 cent per point on something you would have paid for anyway: travel through Scene+ Travel or charged to your Scotiabank card, groceries at participating stores, or Cineplex. Avoid Points for Credit unless you have no other use, because it gives about 0.67 cents per point.

Do Scene+ points expire?

Scene+ points do not expire while your eligible Scotiabank product stays open. If you close it, you remain a member and the points expire after 24 months of inactivity.

Can I transfer Scene+ points to Aeroplan or another program?

No. Scotiabank says Scene+ points are non-transferable.
