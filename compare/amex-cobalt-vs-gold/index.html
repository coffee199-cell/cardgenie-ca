Amex Cobalt vs Gold Rewards Card Canada: Full Comparison

Both cards earn American Express Membership Rewards points, but they target very different spenders. The Cobalt rewards everyday dining and grocery purchases at 5x; the Gold offers a much larger welcome bonus and a flat 2x on a broader set of categories including gas. Here is how they stack up.

Amex Cobalt Wins When...

Cobalt is the stronger card if:

  • You spend heavily at restaurants, grocery stores, or bars — the 5x earn rate is one of the highest in Canada for food categories
  • You prefer a lower committed annual fee — at $12.99/month, you can cancel partway through the year without forfeiting a full annual payment
  • You spend on streaming subscriptions — 3x on Netflix, Spotify, etc. adds up meaningfully
  • You use public transit regularly — 2x on transit beats the Gold's 1x
  • Your monthly spending on food and dining exceeds roughly $500 — at that level, the Cobalt's higher earn rate more than offsets its relative annual fee structure

Amex Gold Wins When...

Gold is the stronger card if:

  • You want a large upfront welcome bonus — 60,000 points worth roughly $600 is a significantly better first-year value than the Cobalt's 15,000 points
  • You drive frequently — 2x on gas is a category the Cobalt does not reward
  • You spend at drugstores and pharmacies regularly — 2x versus 1x on the Cobalt
  • You prefer a single annual fee billed once rather than a monthly charge structure
  • Your spending is more spread across gas, groceries, travel, and drugstore rather than concentrated at restaurants

Verdict

Which card should you choose?

For everyday Canadians who spend most of their discretionary money at restaurants and grocery stores, the Amex Cobalt Card delivers higher ongoing earning power — 5x on food purchases is genuinely exceptional. For someone who wants maximum first-year value or who drives often, the Gold Rewards Card's 60,000-point welcome bonus and 2x on gas tip the balance in its favour, particularly in year one. Both cards share identical travel insurance coverage, so that is not a deciding factor. Choose based on where you spend most.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you transfer Cobalt points to a Gold Rewards account?

Both cards earn American Express Membership Rewards points, which are held in the same Amex MR pool. You cannot combine separate cardholder accounts, but if you hold both cards as a single cardmember, your points accrue into one MR balance and are redeemable together.

Which Amex card has a better welcome offer?

The Amex Gold Rewards Card offers the larger welcome bonus: up to 60,000 MR points (worth approximately $600 at standard redemption rates) versus the Cobalt's maximum of 15,000 points (worth approximately $150) earned at 1,250 points per month over 12 months. If first-year total value is the priority, Gold wins outright.

Which is better for grocery spending?

The Amex Cobalt earns 5x MR points on grocery purchases, compared to the Gold's 2x. On $500 per month in groceries, the Cobalt earns 2,500 points versus 1,000 on the Gold — a meaningful gap that compounds month over month. The Cobalt is the clear winner for grocery-heavy households.
