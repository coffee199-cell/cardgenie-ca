About CardGenie

CardGenie is a Canadian credit card comparison tool. We track 40+ cards in a live database, updated daily, so the numbers you see reflect current fees, earn rates, and welcome offers rather than information that was accurate six months ago.

◆

What CardGenie Is

CardGenie is a Canadian credit card comparison and calculator tool. We maintain a database of 40+ Canadian credit cards — tracking annual fees, earn rates by spending category, welcome bonuses, interest rates, income requirements, and insurance coverage details. The database is updated daily using a combination of automated monitoring of issuer websites and the Government of Canada's Financial Consumer Agency of Canada (FCAC) database, supplemented with manual verification when terms change.

Our calculator takes your actual monthly spending across categories like groceries, gas, dining, travel, and other purchases, and models the net annual value of each card in our database against your numbers. The goal is to surface which card will put the most money back in your pocket given how you actually spend — not based on a hypothetical average Canadian consumer.

◆

How Our Data Works

Our card database is updated on a daily automated schedule. Each entry tracks:

  • Annual fee (and whether it is waived in the first year)
  • Earn rates by spending category
  • Welcome bonus amount, estimated dollar value, and minimum spend requirement
  • Purchase interest rate and cash advance rate
  • Minimum income requirement
  • Travel and purchase insurance details

Every change to a card's terms is logged in a change history, so we can track when issuers adjust rates or promotions. We cross-reference our data against the FCAC's public credit card comparison database to catch discrepancies. When terms change significantly, we flag the affected pages for manual review before publishing updated content.

Despite these processes, credit card terms can change faster than any database can track them. We always recommend verifying current terms directly with the issuer before applying.

How We Make Recommendations

Our calculator models actual expected rewards value for each card in our database based on your specific spending inputs. The calculation works as follows:

1

You enter your monthly spending by category

Groceries, gas, dining, travel, and other categories. This takes about 30 seconds and does not require creating an account.

2

We apply each card's earn rate to your spending

For each card, we apply the broadly available earn rate for each category, enforce monthly and annual bonus limits, and use the base rate after a limit is reached. Retailer-, airline-, and booking-portal bonuses are not assumed from a broad category input.

3

We convert rewards to a dollar value and subtract the annual fee

Points currencies are converted using conservative estimated redemption values, which are not guaranteed cash values. The card's annual fee is subtracted to produce an estimated net annual value.

4

We rank cards by net value for your spending profile

Cards are ranked by estimated net annual value — the card that puts the most money back in your pocket for your specific spending mix appears first. We do not rank by commission, affiliate relationship, or any other factor.

Editorial Standards

CardGenie content is informational only. We do not provide personalized financial advice and nothing on this site should be construed as a recommendation to apply for any specific product. Credit card suitability depends on individual financial circumstances that we cannot assess.

CardGenie currently has no preferred partner relationships, affiliate agreements, or sponsored placements with any credit card issuer. Our rankings and content are not influenced by commercial relationships with card issuers. If affiliate or referral arrangements are established in the future, they will be disclosed clearly on all affected pages and in our site footer.

We aim to present card features accurately based on publicly available information. Where figures represent estimates — such as points valuations — we note this. We do not guarantee that data is error-free or current at the exact moment you read it; always verify with the issuer before making a financial decision.

Who Runs CardGenie

CardGenie is built and maintained by Ryan Billings, an independent site operator based in Ontario. There is no editorial team and no outside ownership. One person writes the comparisons, maintains the card database and runs the daily update jobs, and that person is accountable for everything published here.

To be explicit about what that does and does not mean: Ryan is not a financial advisor, not a licensed professional in any financial field, and does not hold credentials in banking or lending. Nothing on CardGenie is personalized financial advice, and it should not be treated as a substitute for advice from someone qualified to assess your circumstances.

What CardGenie does offer is verifiable method rather than credentials. Every earn rate, fee, cap and interest rate on the site is recorded in a structured database with a source URL and a date attached, cross-referenced against the Government of Canada's Financial Consumer Agency database, and updated on an automated daily schedule. Every change an issuer makes is logged with a timestamp. The dollar figures in comparison articles are generated from that same database using published point valuations, so an article and the calculator cannot quietly disagree with each other.

That approach is deliberate. Credit card advice is easy to write confidently and hard to write accurately, because the details that decide the answer, particularly category caps and how they are shared across categories, are buried in issuer documentation and change without announcement. Recording the data properly and showing the arithmetic is more useful than an opinion, and it is checkable, which an opinion is not.

If you find a figure on this site that is wrong or out of date, please report it to coffee199@gmail.com. Corrections are made to the database, which propagates to every page that uses the figure.
